How it works

Four steps from an empty wallet to a busy space base.

1

Connect a wallet

Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Connecting is read-only and moves no funds.

2

Hold $SORE

The $SORE in your wallet sets how many crew bunks your base has. Every new base also gets a free Grey Drone.

3

Let the crew work

Your aliens fly out to the asteroid belt, the comets and the crystal moon whether you're online or not. The Docking Hall holds 12 hours of haul.

4

Collect the haul

Dock in to collect what your crew brought back. Withdrawals to your wallet open at launch.

The crew

Six aliens, one per rarity. Rarer aliens bring back more per run.

Pods. Each recruit pod gives you one alien; the odds are on every card. Fusion. Three aliens of the same rarity merge into one of the next rarity. Final $SORE prices are posted at launch.

The greenhouse

The hands-on side of the base. Grow space crops under the dome and harvest a little more than you spent. Slower crops return a bit more.
